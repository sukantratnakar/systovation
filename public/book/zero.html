Zero - Before and After Singularity

by Sukant Ratnakar

What happens when artificial intelligence becomes smarter than humans? It's called the singularity, and it might be closer than we think. Sukant Ratnakar doesn't approach this topic with dystopian dread or utopian hype. Instead, he asks honest questions: What does it mean to be at point zero, the moment before everything changes? How do we prepare? What remains uniquely human? The book blends philosophy with practical thinking, making a complex topic feel personal. It's not a tech manual or a sci-fi novel — it's a meditation on change itself. Whether the singularity comes in ten years or a hundred, the questions raised here will stay relevant.
