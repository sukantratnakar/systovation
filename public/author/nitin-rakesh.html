Nitin Rakesh

CEO & Author

Nitin Rakesh runs Mphasis, a global tech company with tens of thousands of employees. But he's not your typical CEO — he actually thinks about the human side of business and isn't afraid to admit when things are hard. His book with Jerry Wind came from leading through genuine crisis and learning in real-time. He has a Wharton MBA, but he's more interesting than that makes him sound. His writing is surprisingly accessible for someone at his level.
