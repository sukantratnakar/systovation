Tanya Eklund

Children's Author

Tanya Eklund is a real estate professional in Calgary who writes children's books on the side — which sounds like an odd combination until you realize both jobs are about helping people find where they belong. Her first book, I'm More Than Just A Warthog, came from watching her own kids navigate the challenges of being different. She wanted to give them (and other children) a story that says: you're enough, exactly as you are.
