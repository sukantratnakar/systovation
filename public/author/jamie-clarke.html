Jamie Clarke

Adventurer & Author

Jamie Clarke has climbed Everest. Twice. He's crossed one of the most inhospitable deserts on Earth. And somehow he's not insufferable about it. Jamie speaks to companies about resilience and risk, but his real gift is making extreme experiences feel relatable. You haven't climbed a mountain, but you've faced your own versions of impossible. His Systovation contributions capture that energy — the belief that ordinary people can do extraordinary things when they commit.
