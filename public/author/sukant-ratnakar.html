Sukant Ratnakar

Founder & Publisher

Sukant Ratnakar has been thinking about how people and organizations evolve for over twenty years. He's written more than thirty books on topics ranging from mindfulness to management, but they all circle back to the same question: how do we grow without losing ourselves? He founded Systovation Publishing because he believes executives have wisdom worth sharing and deserve help getting it onto paper. When he's not writing or consulting, you'll find him in Saskatoon, Canada, probably working on the next Quantraz book.
